# Bloom Universe (Bloomun) - Community Context & Copywriting Guidelines

## 🌟 Tentang Bloom Universe

**Bloom Universe** (singkatan: **Bloomun**) adalah server komunitas Discord yang ramah, hangat, dan interaktif untuk mencari teman baru, membangun relasi/networking, main game bareng (**mabar**), nonton bareng movie/anime (**nobar**), serta nongkrong santai (**ngobrol**) di voice channels.

---

## 🎯 Tone of Voice & Copywriting

- **Gaya Bahasa**: Playful, ramah, Gen-Z / gaming culture, tidak kaku, namun tetap profesional dan rapi (_clean & welcoming_).
- **Fokus Utama**: Menghubungkan orang-orang, menyediakan ruang tanpa toxic untuk bermain game bersama, streaming, dan membangun pertemanan nyata.

## 📝 English Copywriting Blueprint

### 1. Hero Section

- **Headline**: `"The Ultimate Hangout to Game, Chill & Make Real Friends"`
- **Subtitle**: `"Welcome to Bloom Universe! Whether you're looking for a late-night gaming squad, cozy movie watch parties, deep voice chats, or simply a place to vibe, you'll always find your circle here."`
- **CTA 1**: `"JOIN OUR DISCORD"`
- **CTA 2**: `"EXPLORE ECOSYSTEM"`

### 2. About Section

- **Title**: `"More Than Just a Discord Server"`
- **Description**: `"Bloom Universe is built for people who want to expand their circle, find gaming partners, hop into movie watch parties, or simply share late-night banter in voice channels."`
- **Stats**:
  - `Total Community Members`
  - `Members Online Now`
  - `Active in Voice Channels`
- **3 Value Pillars**:
  1. `Squad Up & Play`: Never game alone. Find teammates for Valorant, Mobile Legends, Roblox, Minecraft, or casual party games in seconds.
  2. `Watch & Chill Nights`: Cozy community movie streams, anime watch parties, music jamming sessions, and spontaneous voice lounge hangouts.
  3. `Make Real Friends & Vibe`: A warm, welcoming, and wholesome environment to network, share passions, tell stories, and build genuine friendships.

### 3. Gallery Section

- **Title**: `"Moments & Highlights"`
- **Description**: `"Capturing the funniest voice lounge moments, epic gaming clutches, community tournaments, and creative memories across the Bloom Universe."`

### 4. Sub-Web Ecosystem

- **Title**: `"Explore Our Digital Ecosystem"`
- **Description**: `"Interactive community web apps, photobooths, and gaming portals crafted exclusively for Bloom Universe members."`
- **Portals**:
  1. **Bloom Photobooth**: Snap cute polaroids & custom aesthetic frames with your Discord besties.
  2. **Bloom SMP Server**: Build, survive, and embark on epic quests in our official Minecraft world.
  3. **Bloom Roblox World**: Hang out, play custom mini-games, and explore our community map on Roblox.
  4. **Bloom Store**: Exclusive community merch, collectible server badges, and special perks.
  5. **Bloom Social**: A cozy feed to share memes, fanart, gaming clips, and daily community stories.
  6. **Bloom Bot Dashboard**: Custom music player, leveling leaderboards, economy games, and automated server utilities.

### 5. Social Channels

- **Title**: `"Stay Connected Everywhere"`
- **Description**: `"Never miss a gaming tournament, movie watch party, meme drop, or community announcement. Join the conversation on all our verified channels."`
